The reading column is driven by named tokens in `_sass/_variables.scss`. Use them; do not introduce fresh magic numbers for widths.

- Breakpoints: `$medium` 46em (736px) is the phone to tablet switch, deliberately below 48em so iPad mini portrait (744px) and split-view widths get the tablet layout. `$large` 62.5em (1000px) is the desktop switch. `$nav-full` 33.75em (540px) only brings back Weekly and About in the header: Android tablets in portrait sit around 600 to 735px (OPPO Pad Mini), below `$medium`, and showed the phone menu with a third of the bar empty (2026-10-31). Weekly's header uses the same 540px as its Tailwind `nav` screen; change the two together.
- Column: `$page-width` 900px on desktop, `$page-width-tablet` 760px between `$medium` and `$large`, with `$page-gutter` 20px of minimum breathing room. The pattern for a card is `width: calc(100% - #{$page-gutter * 2}); max-width: $page-width-tablet; margin: auto`.
- Desktop type is a five-rung reading ladder (maintainer 2026-11-01, Kata `site.md` B1): 30 titles (post, home list, read-more cards), 24 post `h2` plus the archive years and Notes title, 20 post `h3` and the pager, 17 body and the bold header menu, 14 meta (dates, chips, Notes footers, search hints). Body `h2` / `h3` are set explicitly at every width; the browser's 1.5em / 1.17em gave 25.5 and 19.89. Outside it: the banner verse SVG, the video duration pill. Phones keep their own sizes, except the post title, which is 26 there so it never sits below its 24 `h2`. Post spacing follows the reading rhythm MiaoYan's preview, its iPhone reader and Weekly share (maintainer 2026-11-04): line height 1.64 and 0.02em tracking; `h2` 1.4em above and 0.4em below, `h3` 1.3em and 0.4em, and the block after a heading loses its top margin; paragraphs, lists, quotes, tables and code blocks 1.15em apart, list items 0.4em; quotes in muted `#6e6658` beside a 3px warm `#ddd9cd` bar; inline code in the text face at 1em on a sand tint; code blocks keep their dark Rouge frame with monospace code at 0.86em and line height 1.6; tables 0.92em with 8px 12px cells; content images 8px corners.
- The post body, comments, read-more, index list and header container all track the same column, so their left edges line up. Change one and change the rest.
- The reading column is centred on the viewport at every width, including when the table of contents shows. The rail lives in the right margin, so the contents only appear from 94.25em (1508px), the width where a centred 900px column still leaves `$toc-rail` plus `$page-gutter` on the right. Below that the rail is hidden rather than shrunk or shifted (maintainer call 2026-08: a centred column is worth more than showing contents on 1366 and 1440). Do not reintroduce `padding-right: $toc-rail` on `#main` / `.header-menu`: that centred column and rail as a pair, which kept the rail on screen from 1344px but pushed the column 142px left of centre on every desktop width, and reading it as "off-centre" is exactly what got it changed.

- Styles and vendor files nothing produces or loads are deleted, not kept for later (2026-10-29: the 404 robot, the theme's button variants, the Google 404 box, `_grid.scss` and twelve reveal files PPT pages never request). Before deleting a selector whose class is missing from `_site`, also search `js/` and the notes JSON in `_site/notes/html/`, which endless scroll injects; keep the Rouge token colours, kramdown's `.footnotes` and element rules Markdown can write even when no post uses them yet. Prove it with computed styles of every element on the home page, a post, about, 404, notes and a PPT post before and after.
