# Yado

> Native Mac app for managing an iPhone over USB: clean up photos and apps, move files, and keep local backups.

Website: https://yado.house

Yado is a commercial product by [Tw93](https://tw93.fun). Yado is a paid native Mac app at https://yado.house for managing an iPhone from a Mac. It clears duplicate photos, blurry shots, old screenshots, large videos and unused apps, exports originals to the Mac and verifies them before anything is deleted, arranges the Home Screen, moves files both ways, and keeps incremental local backups that can be browsed, exported and restored. Photos, exports and backups stay on the Mac or the connected iPhone. This is a closed source commercial product, so there is no public repository.

License: Proprietary

## Key Features

- Overview: device, battery and storage details, cable speed, backup management, screenshots and screen recording
- Clean: duplicate photos, blurry shots, clutter and old screenshots, all confirmed before anything changes
- Photos: browse by album or place, export originals or JPG to the Mac, and verify local copies before deletion
- Apps: inspect storage use, uninstall selected apps, organize the Home Screen and save or restore its layouts
- Files: browse accessible folders and file-sharing apps' Documents, preview, upload and export
- Backups: incremental and scheduled local backups with encryption controls, validation and restore; browse and export contacts, notes and messages
- Hardware Check: battery authenticity, replaced parts and restart history, saved as one picture

## FAQ

**How much does Yado cost?**

$19 once for two Macs, with all future updates. Each action can be tried twice for free, and there is a 14-day full refund.

**What does Yado need to run?**

A Mac on macOS 14 or later and an iPhone. Connect the unlocked iPhone by USB and accept Trust the first time. After Connect over Wi-Fi is turned on, the overview, apps, files and backups also work over Wi-Fi; photo-library access, restore, screenshots and screen recording need a cable.

**Does Yado work with iPad, Android or Windows?**

No. Yado supports iPhone only and runs on the Mac. It does not need a jailbreak, and it reads what is stored on the phone, so iCloud-only originals that are not on the phone are not exported over USB.

**Where does Yado keep my photos and backups?**

On the Mac or the connected iPhone. Yado does not ask for an Apple Account or retain backup passwords, and the installed app list never leaves the Mac. Full details are at https://yado.house/privacy.

**Is Yado open source?**

No. Yado is a closed source commercial product. Guides, bug reports and feature requests live at https://github.com/tw93/Yado-App, which holds no source code.
